# Classification Heuristics

Use these rules when evolving or inspecting the skill:

- Infer one of `general`, `humanities`, `party_building`.
- Also infer audience, purpose, and technical depth.
- Category decides Tsinghua asset family.
- Purpose decides whether the deck should feel like a proposal, progress report, lecture, architecture review, or summary.
- Technical depth should increase visualization pressure rather than increasing slide count.
